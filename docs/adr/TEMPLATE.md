# ADR-NNN: Title (imperative summary of the decision)

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Plan reference:** PLAN §N

## Context

What situation forces a decision? Constraints, not preferences. Two or three
sentences.

## Decision

What we will do, stated so a future session can act on it without reading
anything else.

## Alternatives rejected

Each alternative with the one reason that killed it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we are explicitly accepting
(including known gaps), and what would trigger revisiting this ADR.
